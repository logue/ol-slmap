# OpenLayers — Complete Official Examples Catalog & Recipe Matrix 📚🗺️

> Exhaustive index of all **281 official OpenLayers examples** from [`openlayers.org/en/latest/examples/`](https://openlayers.org/en/latest/examples/) cross-referenced with MapSnippets production recipes in [`skills/openlayers/examples/`](../examples/INDEX.md).

---

## 🧭 Catalog Architecture & Taxonomy

The 281 official examples are organized across 7 core GIS and WebGL domains:
1. **Vector Formats, MVT & Cartography** (GeoJSON, KML, TopoJSON, MVT, styling)
2. **Projections & Advanced Datums** (EPSG:4326, EPSG:3857, Swiss LV95, British National Grid)
3. **Digitization & GIS Interactions** (Draw, Modify, Snap, Select, Translate, DragBox)
4. **Clustering, Heatmaps & Dense WebGL** (Cluster, Heatmap, WebGLPoints, WebGLTile)
5. **Enterprise OGC Services** (WMS, WMTS, WFS, ArcGIS REST)
6. **Raster, WebGL, COG & GeoZarr** (Cloud-Optimized GeoTIFF, GeoZarr, Band Math, Shaders)
7. **UI Controls & Navigation** (OverviewMap, ZoomSlider, Swipe, FlyTo, ScaleLine)
